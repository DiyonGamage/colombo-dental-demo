About the clinic

A dental clinic built around restorative work.

Colombo Dental Clinics opened on Havelock Road in 2008. Alongside everyday dentistry, we focus on restoring teeth — crowns, bridges, implants and full smile rebuilds.

Having the lab in the same building changes how that works. The dentist and the technician look at the same case together, so the shade, the fit and the bite are checked before you leave the chair.

  • Digital scanning and shade matching on site
  • Zirconia and ceramic crowns made in our lab
  • Two locations, one team, one set of records

Questions

Before you book.

Where are your clinics?
Our main clinic and dental lab are at 493 Havelock Road, Colombo 06 (Pamankada). Our second clinic is at 14 Trelawney Place, off Bauddhaloka Mawatha, Colombo 04 (Bambalapitiya).
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Friday, 9.00am to 7.00pm. Saturday, 9.00am to 12.30pm. Closed on Sunday. For pain or a broken tooth, message us on WhatsApp — same-day slots are often available.
Do I need an appointment?
It is best to book first so you are not kept waiting. Message 071 922 0000 on WhatsApp or call 011 732 0075 and we will give you the next available time.
Do you make crowns and bridges on site?
Yes. Our Pamankada clinic has its own dental laboratory, so crowns, bridges and zirconia work are made in the same building. That usually means fewer visits and a better fit.
Do you do implants and braces?
Yes. We place dental implants and provide both metal braces and clear aligners. We also do root canal treatment, extractions, whitening and bonding.
How do I know what it will cost?
You will be given the cost and the number of visits before treatment starts. If a plan needs to change once we start, we will tell you first — not after.
